# Prelocked V14 same-checkpoint identity-toggle diagnostic

Locked while V14 full DEV is still RUNNING, before reading its selected DEV scores or this diagnostic. This is a secondary mechanism check using only saved **training** counterfactual states/Q banks. It is neither a new method, an independent evaluation set, nor confirmatory TEST evidence, and cannot promote V14 alone.

## Question and estimand

At each V14 `competition` training state, with the exact pre-update actor weights, current observation and legal matching set fixed, does scoring by the joint unserved-identity formula select a lower common-bank H64 root cost than scoring by the count-calibrated formula? Instantiate both modes with bit-identical tensors from the old `competition` checkpoint; leave edge, demand and WAIT heads, state, legal mask and Q bank unchanged. Let `a_joint` and `a_count` be deterministic catalogue-tie-broken argmax actions. Primary diagnostic `D = Q(a_joint) − Q(a_count)` on the saved four-scenario bank; negative is favorable to the joint identity term. The bank uses old joint-policy continuation after the forced root, so this isolates only the root decision under that continuation. It is not the closed-loop effect of deploying count mode or retraining it.

Use every `competition` Q row: five train seeds 72600000–04 × six updates × four cases × 64 hash-selected states = 7,680 rows. No extra seed, DEV episode, TEST, new forecast or adaptive selection. Check complete source grid, source/artifact/old-checkpoint hashes and legal action reconstruction before publishing. Equal-weight mean D over all rows is primary, with per-training-seed, case and update means and paired hierarchical 20,000-draw 95% CI resampling five training seeds then state ranks within each case/update. A directional signal requires mean D ≤−5, negative mean in ≥4/5 training seeds and CI upper <0. This is deliberately weaker than overall goal success.

Secondary diagnostics: changed action fraction and type (WAIT, serviced-machine set, technician assignment), improved/worsened/tied rows, |D|≥5 count, mean |D|, service cardinality in each mode, and the fraction of states where at least two legal two-service matchings have nonidentical joint-minus-count penalties. Also report the same cost metrics on the changed-action subset, labeled selected/associational. Because the two modes agree exactly for WAIT and one-service actions at matched weights, changed choices must involve a two-service matching; verify this invariant. Record any violation as audit failure.

Single complete pass, no tuning or repeat on alternate state samples. Run only after V14 source manifest is `COMPLETED` and its independent main audit passes. If incomplete, fail and retain the timestamped artifact. Output a new `research_result/v14_identity_toggle_{smoke,dev}_<UTC>` directory refusing overwrite: `rows.csv` with source identity, two actions, both Q values, D, decision class/cardinality and opportunity flag; `summary.json` with locked metrics/CI; `manifest.json` with source run/manifest/Q/selection/checkpoint and script/protocol hashes, exact row count, status and runtime. Independent verifier reconstructs all choices/Q differences, the mechanism formula and summary/bootstrap from immutable rows. Ubuntu smoke on the V14 smoke artifact and focused tests precede commit/push of diagnostic source; full diagnostic uses its own new timestamped directory, tqdm and a 20-minute timeout.
