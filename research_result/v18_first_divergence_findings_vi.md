## Interpretation and next decision

V18's dual-continuation blend produced no stable matched-capacity ablation gain and remains +2.26% above rollout, beyond the original 1%/10× route despite 41.4× faster decision time. The present diagnostic narrows the unresolved issue to **repeated commitment and continuation consistency**: a root can look nearly tied to rollout under V18's future policy, but worse under a risk continuation; first-root estimates fail to account for most closed-loop cost. A new method should explicitly control how a service commitment changes future feasible requests and the policy's later choices, and must ablate that mechanism against an equal-capacity/compute controller plus simple risk/rollout/V14 baselines. Do not promote V18, claim novelty, open fresh TEST or infer benefit from this post hoc probe. Prelock any V19 proposal, metrics, seed splits and stopping rule before scratch-RL training.
