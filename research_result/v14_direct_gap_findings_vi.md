Other first-divergence categories can also matter, so this category is not a complete closed-loop causal decomposition. This is an opened-DEV diagnosis and cannot by itself demonstrate a new method or support TEST.

**Decision.** Use the V14 family as the strongest near-rollout structured scratch-RL starting point, while treating its identity-specific opportunity term as unproven (prior matched-capacity ablation failed). Before a new method, inspect its already logged training Q banks and selected-policy logits at early states to distinguish training-credit bias from poor WAIT/service calibration or coverage. Then prelock a distinct V14-anchored method targeting long-horizon service-start decisions, with matched-capacity ablation, fresh five-seed scratch-RL DEV, rollout and flat PPO comparisons, independent replication and only one eligible fresh frozen TEST. Keep original A/B competition thresholds; no V14 promotion from this diagnostic.
