# V17 prior-art boundary before DEV outcomes

This memo was drafted while the V17 scratch-RL campaign was still training; no V17 DEV selection/final scores had been read. It narrows the novelty interpretation and does not alter the frozen campaign or source hashes.

- [Learning to Dispatch (NeurIPS 2020)](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/file/11958dfee29b6709f48a9ba0387a2431-Paper.pdf) already demonstrates scratch-RL learning of structured dispatch rules with shared graph representation and scale transfer in job-shop scheduling. A V17 result cannot claim the general idea of learned dispatching, structured policy or scratch training.
- [Wang and Gombolay, ICAPS 2022 / AirME](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/19839) already addresses stochastic predictive maintenance resource allocation using heterogeneous graph state and decision-value nodes plus a policy optimization augmentation. A V17 result cannot claim first RL for shared maintenance or first decision-value representation.
- [Hybrid MPO (PMLR 2020)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v100/neunert20a/neunert20a.pdf) explicitly constructs improved action distributions from Q and KL-constrained projection; [Muesli (PMLR 2021)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/hessel21a/hessel21a.pdf) and [Regularized MDP theory (PMLR 2019)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geist19a/geist19a-supp.pdf) likewise establish softmax/regularized policy improvement. V17's relative-Q softmax target and trust penalty are instances of established ideas, not a novel generic RL optimizer. V17 uses exhaustive legal matching Q banks with simulator common shocks and a fixed temperature rather than claiming the exact MPO dual or theoretical guarantee.
- [Value Function Based Production Scheduling](https://publications.ri.cmu.edu/value-function-based-production-scheduling) establishes older value-guided production scheduling. A V17 result cannot claim invention of value-function scheduling.

The only potentially defensible contribution is an empirically successful, carefully controlled **domain-specific formulation** for persistent requests, heterogeneous technician compatibility and nonpreemptive commitments: exact legal partial-matching root Q over H48+tail, old-policy common-scenario counterfactuals, shared full-matching scorer and low-latency deployment. Even that requires fair ablation versus the same-capacity expected-Q control and strong planner/heuristic baselines, independent replication, fresh TEST and evidence that the design choice (not capacity or compute) drives gain. If V17 is merely an off-the-shelf projection objective in this small simulator, report it as an application/negative or incremental result; do not assert research-method novelty by renaming it.
