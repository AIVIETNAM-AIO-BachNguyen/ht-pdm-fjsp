# V20 prospective primary-literature boundary

Targeted primary-source check on 2026-10-21 before V20 method implementation. This is not exhaustive and establishes no novelty claim by itself.

- [Zhang et al., Learning to Dispatch for Job Shop Scheduling via Deep Reinforcement Learning, NeurIPS 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12367) learn dispatching rules from scratch with a disjunctive-graph policy. Learning a scheduling policy from raw states or using RL to replace heuristics is established.
- [Wang and Gombolay, Stochastic Resource Optimization over Heterogeneous Graph Neural Networks for Failure-Predictive Maintenance Scheduling, ICAPS 2022](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/download/19839/19598/23852) address whether and when to perform predictive maintenance under stochastic failures and shared crews using HetGPO/AirME. Maintenance timing, repair-resource competition, graph RL and a learned global scheduling policy are established. V20 cannot claim these as new.
- [Bacon, Harb and Precup, The Option-Critic Architecture, AAAI 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05140) learns option policies and termination. Separating an activation/deferral decision from a conditional action is not new as a generic hierarchical RL idea.
- [Tong et al., Combinatorial Optimization Meets Reinforcement Learning: Effective Taxi Order Dispatching at Large-Scale, IEEE TKDE 2023, author copy](https://dingyuan-shi.github.io/resources/TKDE21.pdf) combine learned future resource value with matching and can leave an immediately matchable resource idle. Reserving a resource for possible future demand is established.
- The repository's own V14/V18/V19 campaigns already test joint opportunity penalties, dual-continuation Q credit and temporal matching deficits; V14 identity-specific benefit, V18 continuation correction and V19 material gate failed. V20 must not relabel any of these components as a new contribution.

Potentially defensible *narrow* question: in repeated-request shared-technician maintenance, can a scratch-RL matching policy learn a counterfactual **whole-matching activation margin** against WAIT, using the same action bank and capacity as an expected-Q control, and thereby avoid long-horizon over-commitment while retaining reciprocal3 service quality? This is an empirical method hypothesis, not a novelty conclusion. If its matched ablation, competitive gate, replication or fresh TEST fails, no performance or novelty claim follows. A later positive result would still require deeper related-work search around binary start/stop gating, structured action policies and counterfactual policy improvement.
