# V18 primary prior-art boundary (reviewed during DEV training)

This is a literature boundary, not a change to the locked V18 method, metrics, seeds or stopping rule. No V18 final DEV outcome was read for this note.

- [Learning to Dispatch, NeurIPS 2020](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/11958dfee29b6709f48a9ba0387a2431-Paper.pdf) already learns a dispatching policy from scratch with a graph representation and tests larger JSSP instances. We cannot claim that scratch RL for dispatch, graph scheduling, or size generalization is new here.
- [Wang and Gombolay, ICAPS 2022 / AirME / HetGPO](https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/19839/19839-40-23852-1-2-20220613.pdf), pp. 3–5, models a **collection** of simultaneous maintenance decisions, all planes and crews, a global summary node, and decision-value nodes. It uses PPO with a step-based return baseline and same-instance rollouts for variance reduction. Thus simultaneous resource assignment, global-state coupling, stochastic maintenance RL, and shared stochastic instances are established prior work. Its crew team is homogeneous in the reported AirME instantiation; the paper explicitly leaves heterogeneous crews for future work, but that alone is not a novelty claim for our fixed-size simulator.
- V18's proposed distinction, if the prelocked ablation and competitive replication support it, is narrower: compare full-H root costs under the current actor and a risk continuation on **matched stochastic tapes**, center their action-relative difference inside the legal matching set, and clip its contribution to the learned rank target while retaining a soft service-flow check. This is a method hypothesis, not a proven novelty claim. Q-based policy improvement, KL penalties and conservative updates are also established (see [Hybrid MPO](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v100/neunert20a/neunert20a.pdf) and [CPO](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/achiam17a.html)).

The review above directly checked the two named dispatch/maintenance primary papers and the linked policy-improvement papers in the existing V18 proposal; it is not an exhaustive prior-art search. Any final claim must be scoped to the actual achieved comparison and checked against closer continuation/robust-policy-evaluation literature before publication.
