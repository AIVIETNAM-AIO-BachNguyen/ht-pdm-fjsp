Interpretation: early decisions carry material long-horizon cost, including machine-set and cardinality choices as well as WAIT. A future RL method should learn a state-dependent resource-reservation/continuation decision rather than impose a fixed WAIT duration or copy a single planner action. The diagnostic still leaves roughly half the planner advantage after one oracle correction and fails its seed-robust gate. V12 remains a DEV no-go; goal remains **ACTIVE**. A further prelocked diagnostic can test whether a small *budgeted sequence* of planner corrections reaches the 1% cost route and how many planner queries its oracle requires; only then is an RL consultation/option design worth implementing. No claim of novelty or scalability follows from this intervention.
