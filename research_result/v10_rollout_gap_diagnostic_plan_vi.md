# Prelocked V10 selected-policy versus rollout action/value diagnostic (DEV only)

Locked after independently audited V10 DEV no-go `bf9ae2f` and before this diagnostic's implementation/outcomes. This reuses already-opened V10 DEV physical seeds and immutable selected checkpoints; it is a mechanism probe, not TEST, replication, new method or planner optimality claim. The V10 method's largest cost gaps to the six-tick bounded rollout were +73.46 in interchangeable scale1 and +91.42 in reciprocal scale2, driven in aggregate by more failures despite similar service starts. That does not identify whether timing, machine priority or technician assignment caused the gap.

**Hypotheses.** H1: in interchangeable scale1 and reciprocal scale2, V10 often chooses a different served-machine set or timing than same-state bounded rollout, with a ≥5-unit six-tick root-value gap. H2: technician assignment is instead the main gap, despite V10's assignment-specific term. H3: same-state root-value gaps are too small to explain full-episode difference, suggesting longer-horizon or state-distribution effects. No category is assumed true.

**Population.** Replay every selected V10 DEV episode: four cases × two V10 arms × five training seeds × fixed 16 V10 DEV physical seeds 70410000–15 = 640 episodes and 30,720 H decisions. Check each episode objective, operational metrics and request conservation against immutable V10 `episodes.csv`. At every pre-action state record V10 and risk choices, legal matching set, queued CM/PM, free technicians, action cardinality/machine set/assignment, chosen and risk known duration, tick bin, and WAIT with/without any legal service. Use the V9 mutually exclusive taxonomy: same, WAIT/service, lower/higher cardinality, different machine set, different technician assignment. This full trace describes V10's visited distribution; it does not infer rollout's own distribution or label risk disagreement an error.

**Bounded root-value sample.** For states with ≥2 legal actions, hash-rank `(version,case,source arm,training seed,physical seed,tick)` within each case×arm×training-seed×tick-bin (0–15,16–31,32–47), keep the first two. Maximum 240 states; sparse strata stay sparse. A **new independent forecast anchor 70460000** scores every legal root with the unchanged 16-scenario, six-tick, threshold-requester, risk-continuation bounded rollout, one common bank per state and risk-first tie. Compare the source arm, same-seed other V10 arm, risk and bounded planner on the **same state**; report root regret distributions at ≥1/5/10, action class, case/seed/bin, and whether the rollout best action is excluded by any heuristic mask. Smoke uses the first physical DEV seed, one state/stratum and one forecast scenario. The root value is a current-information finite-scenario estimate, not actual H+tail regret or causal episode impact. No realized future information enters choices.

**Decision gate.** If the two target cases have frequent ≥5 root gaps in a specific timing/machine-subset/assignment class across at least four of five V10 seeds, prelock one RL mechanism for that class and an equal-compute scratch-RL ablation. If few root gaps appear despite large closed-loop cost, investigate long-horizon credit or state-distribution differences before V11. No TEST or result claim from this diagnostic alone. The unchanged competitive goal and its prior-work, independent-replication and fresh-confirmation requirements remain in force.
