Locked after independent V14 direct-gap audit and before inspecting old V14 training Q scores or selected actor logits in this probe. No new RL training, future TEST or new physical episodes. Source is the immutable audited `framework_v14_dev_20261020T185303Z/counterfactual_states.csv` plus frozen selected V14 competition checkpoints. The question is whether V14's harmful early service starts arise because its stochastic old-policy training Q favors service, because the final actor does not follow Q evidence, or because the training state distribution omits the relevant WAIT/service choice.

Use **all** competition-arm training rows in interchangeable_scale1 and reciprocal_scale2, across five training seeds and six updates. Primary subset: tick <16, WAIT legal and at least one non-WAIT matching legal. For each row, parse the stored full legal-root Q bank and reconstruct the current observation from its saved state. Verify source/checkpoint hashes, case, legal set and request state. Compute ΔQ = min non-WAIT Q − Q_WAIT (positive means WAIT better), WAIT rank, source-action category, frozen selected actor's greedy action and masked WAIT probability. Report case, seed and iteration counts, fractions ΔQ>0, ΔQ≥5, actor selects WAIT when ΔQ≥5, actor selects service when ΔQ≥5, and vice versa for ΔQ≤−5, Q regret of selected action, and empirical ΔQ mean/quantiles. Report same metrics for all ticks as a coverage check. Describe first-divergence tick/category distribution from the independently audited direct-gap result; do not call training states matched to evaluation states merely because they share a tick.

Hypothesis H_credit: training Q mostly favors service at early legal WAIT/service states even when the new diagnostic greedy continuation favors WAIT on its distinct first-divergence states; this would motivate changing continuation/credit. H_calibration: training Q commonly favors WAIT by ≥5 yet frozen selected actor still chooses service in a material fraction; this would motivate WAIT-relative action learning or optimization. Neither conclusion follows unless coverage and selection effects are reported. No policy promotion or TEST from this retrospective probe.
