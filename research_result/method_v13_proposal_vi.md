# V13 proposal: learned technician opportunity cost over unserved machines

**Problem and evidence.** V12's independently audited RL cost 904.08 still trails bounded rollout 849.56. On immutable DEV, its first actor/planner divergence often changes WAIT, machine set or cardinality early; one oracle correction recovers 44.8%/50.7% of two target case gaps, yet fixed WAIT and sparse direct consultation fail seed robustness, remaining cost or runtime. The current shared-edge actor sums independent machine–technician logits. Its edge score has no explicit dependence on *which other machines remain unserved under this candidate matching*, so it cannot directly price occupying a technician while another machine's request/failure risk is rising. V8 found this structural limitation but did not show frequent large six-tick context flips; V10–V12 show longer-horizon timing/competition effects. Flat PPO sees all 56 features but has no matching-specific sharing.

**Idea and formulation.** Keep the same threshold requester, all legal matchings including WAIT, four cases and H48+tail cost. A scratch RL actor embeds every current machine, technician and service edge from the same 56 observation coordinates as flat PPO. For each candidate matching `a`, it scores selected edges and subtracts a learned *occupation × unserved-demand* term: `L(a)=Σ_(m,j)∈a u(m,j,global) − Σ_j [duration_j(a) × Σ_(n not served by a) d(n,j,global)] + 1[a=WAIT]w(global)`. Here `d` is nonnegative and learned from current ages/failures/request queues, worker state and edge eligibility/duration; `duration_j(a)` is known current service time. This supplies candidate-specific opportunity cost for committing shared technician time while another machine remains exposed. The pairwise term makes matching scores nonadditive. All quantities use current information; no future event tape or planner action enters inference. The actor is evaluated across every legal matching and trained from scratch with same H64 old-actor counterfactual Q update and λ=.5 conditional assignment term as V12. A matched ablation sets only the occupation × unserved-demand term to zero, while retaining the same encoders/global summaries, seeds, Q budget and optimizer. The term is trainable only in the proposed arm; report effective parameter difference. Flat PPO and prior structured/heuristic/planner baselines remain locked.

**Why this fits and closest prior work.** Technician service durations make resource occupancy explicit, and machines without current requests still carry age/failure exposure. [Learning to Dispatch](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/hash/11958dfee29b6709f48a9ba0387a2431-Abstract.html) learns job-shop dispatching with a graph representation; [HetGPO/AirME](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/19839) already learns resource-constrained predictive-maintenance scheduling; [Expected Policy Gradients](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v21/18-012.html) already integrates action values. The defensible proposal is the *specific matching-conditioned opportunity-cost mechanism and its ablation in this shared-technician simulator*, not novelty of GNNs, RL dispatch, actor sharing or exhaustive action integration. A learned shadow price and action interaction may overlap broader scheduling literature; novelty remains unproven pending further primary-source comparison.

**Falsifiable hypotheses.** H1: the competition term improves fresh five-seed case-balanced DEV cost by ≥10 versus same-architecture no-competition ablation, with positive gain in ≥4/5 seeds including all three strong ablation seeds. H2: it reduces the early interchangeable/reciprocal2 rollout gap without excessive under-service and reaches ≤1.02× rollout with ≥10× selection speed and no simple heuristic cost/runtime dominance. If it only rescues weak seeds, produces fixed WAIT-like behavior or leaves the planner gap, reject it. Any DEV promotion would require a fresh locked TEST and independent replication before the goal's A/B claim.

**Ablations and controls.** Same architecture and current-information input, with/without the explicit action-conditioned opportunity-cost term; paired initial weights and first-iteration physical seed roles; state trajectories may diverge immediately because the opportunity-cost term is active at initialization, so scenario banks are paired only conditional on a shared state; same per-state H64 scenario count and state-selection quota, optimizer and checkpoint rule; actual selected states are logged and can differ by arm. Record action types, early divergence and per-case queue/failure consequences. Also compare flat PPO, V7 structured policies, FCFS, risk matching and bounded rollout. Capacity/compute and per-action inference time are reported. No imitation, pretraining, shortest-duration hard mask or planner calls in deployed actor.
