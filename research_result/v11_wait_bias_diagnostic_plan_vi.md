# Prelocked V11 continuation-bias action/Q diagnostic (DEV only)

Locked after independently audited V11 no-go `87249d7` and before this diagnostic implementation/outcomes. It only reuses immutable V11 selected/initial checkpoints, training Q banks and already-opened DEV physical seeds. It is a mechanism diagnosis, not a new method, TEST, replication or confirmation. V11 Q_ref updating cost 1138.76 versus self-Q 946.45; reference seeds01/03 selected untrained iteration0 at 1374.20, with very low service starts/availability and high CM queues. The cause could be continuation inconsistency, Q scenario variance, actor optimization or initial weakness.

**Hypotheses.** H1: on states where legal service exists, the stored four-scenario `Q_ref` more often favors WAIT over the best service matching than `Q_self`, including cases with a ≥5 cost difference; this is especially prevalent in reference seeds01/03. H2: reference-trained or selected policies WAIT more with legal service than matched self-credit policies and have low service starts/large queues on the same DEV physical tapes. Joint H1+H2 supports a continuation-inconsistency mechanism, but not proof that the Q preference alone caused the full cost. If Q preferences or actions do not align, investigate scenario noise/initialization or matching representation instead.

**Population and metrics.** Read every 7,680 stored V11 training state Q pair from `framework_v11_dev_20261020T115151Z/counterfactual_states.csv`; verify complete 2 arms×5 seeds×6 iterations×4 cases×32 hash-selected states and source/manifest hashes. For each row with WAIT and ≥1 legal service root, compute `Q_self(WAIT)−min_service Q_self` and `Q_ref(WAIT)−min_service Q_ref`, signed so negative favors WAIT. Count negative/<−5/positive/>+5 by arm, seed, iteration, case and early/middle/late tick bin. Compare signs on the *same bank*, and report mean difference, disagreement rate and relation to sampled source action. Do not treat a four-scenario Q as true optimal value.

Replay deterministic policies for every selected and iteration0 checkpoint of both V11 arms on four cases×16 fixed V11 DEV seeds: 1,280 episode slots (selected may equal iteration0; retain both labels). At each H decision record WAIT with legal nonempty matching, service starts/cardinality and disagreement with same-state risk. Verify every replay objective, cost components, H availability/failures/CM/PM queues, starts and request conservation against immutable `development.csv` and selected `episodes.csv` as applicable. Initial actor weights are paired by seed across arms; initial episodes should match exactly. No source actor is changed.
