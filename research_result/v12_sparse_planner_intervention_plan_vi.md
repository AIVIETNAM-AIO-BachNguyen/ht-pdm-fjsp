# Prelock: V12 first-divergence planner-intervention diagnosis (opened DEV only)

Locked after V12 independently audited no-go `78843fd` and before this diagnostic implementation/outcomes. This tests whether the bounded planner's advantage in interchangeable scale1 and reciprocal scale2 is localized to a pivotal decision or requires sustained control. It is **not** a deployable controller, a new RL method, fresh evaluation, TEST or independent replication. The planner action is supplied by an oracle first-divergence finder; no learned policy can access that trigger without future work.

**Hypothesis and alternatives.** A single correctly timed planner matching at the first actor/planner disagreement may substantially reduce the actor's full-episode cost; if not, four consecutive WAIT decisions after a planner-WAIT first disagreement may recover a meaningful extra fraction by avoiding the actor's immediate reversal. If neither works, the rollout gap is distributed across later decisions/state distributions, and sparse one-call or fixed WAIT-option designs lack support. Do not presume WAIT or shortest-duration assignment is always optimal.

**Fixed population and paired interventions.** Use immutable selected V12 reliability-credit checkpoints for all five train seeds 71500000–04 and the already-opened DEV physical tapes 71510000–15, in two target cases: interchangeable scale1 and reciprocal scale2 (160 actor/planner pairs). At each actor trajectory, compare deterministic actor and locked V7 bounded rollout on the *same state* until their first different action; this is the first divergence of the two policies on that tape. Replay from episode start on the same physical seed in three arms: unchanged actor, force the one planner action at this first divergence then resume actor, and, only when that planner action is WAIT, force WAIT for four ticks starting there before resuming actor; otherwise the third arm equals the one-action arm. Continue fixed threshold requester and unchanged H48+tail physics. Verify actor and stored rollout objective/operational metrics against immutable V12 `episodes.csv`; no planner call occurs after the first divergence within either intervention. Store every chosen action and first divergence class. A `no_divergence` pair has identical three actor arms.

**Primary metrics/gates.** For each case and train seed report mean objective of actor, one-action, four-tick and stored full rollout on the same physical tapes; actor−rollout gap and recovered fraction `(actor−intervention)/(actor−rollout)` where denominator positive, otherwise report raw differences. Also report H failures, availability, queued CM/PM ticks, starts, cost parts, first-divergence tick/action class, share of planner-WAIT divergences, and planner forecast transitions used to find the oracle trigger. One-action concentration is supported only if ≥30% of positive mean actor-rollout gap is recovered in both cases and in ≥4/5 seeds per case. WAIT-option support requires ≥20 percentage points further recovery over one-action in reciprocal2 and no worsening >5 cost units in interchangeable. Otherwise reject those sparse designs. These are directional diagnostic gates, not completion thresholds or claims of deployability.

**Workflow.** Focused schedule tests and a one-seed/one-physical/two-case Ubuntu smoke; commit/push verified source/protocol/tests before fixed full DEV. New UTC timestamped artifact directory, refuse overwrite; 10-minute smoke/30-minute full timeout, tqdm over 160 pairs, status and source/checkpoint/V12 hash manifest, action/divergence/episode CSVs, summary. Independent verifier replays all actor/intervention actions and metrics, checks baseline source/selection hashes and summary before report. No TEST, SSH, rsync or user-file changes. Next method choice follows the prelocked gate; do not tune the intervention after seeing outcomes.
