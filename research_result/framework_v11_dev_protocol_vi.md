# V11 reference-continuation scratch-RL DEV protocol

Locked 2026-10-20 before V11 implementation/outcomes. Proposal: `method_v11_proposal_vi.md`. Exploratory fresh DEV only. Frozen V7 four cases, threshold requester, N4/K2 current-information vector, 21 full matching actions, free feasibility mask, H48+tail16 economic objective and shared-edge 1,597-parameter actor. Both V11 arms are scratch RL: `reference_credit` uses complete H64 root returns under deterministic risk continuation; `self_credit` uses complete H64 root returns under the current actor continuation. Both receive the exact same complete physical scenario Q_ref and Q_self banks and compute; both use all-action expected policy-gradient update, no V10 assignment auxiliary. Runtime decisions remain actor-only. Comparators on identical DEV physical seeds: immutable five-seed V7 flat/free, shared/free and shared/canonical, FCFS, risk matching and 16-scenario six-tick bounded rollout. No changed physics, requester, penalty or tail.

**Seed split and training.** Five new independent actor/training seeds 70500000–04; fresh DEV physical seeds 70510000–15; forecast anchor 70520000; bootstrap 70540000; smoke 70590000–01, all checked disjoint from historical registries. Paired initial actor weights and arm-independent physical training seed derivation. Six fixed updates plus iteration0. Per seed/arm/update collect 16 stochastic on-policy source episodes per case (64 total, H48) and hash-select up to 32 multi-action states per case, quotas **16 early ticks 0–15, 8 middle 16–31, 8 late 32–47**. Preserve sparse-bin shortfalls without replacement. This early weighting was fixed from V10 first-divergence timing, not V11 outcomes. For each selected state and every legal root, use four independent paired physical scenarios through H64; the same physical events feed Q_self and Q_ref, and a separate independent policy-uniform stream feeds Q_self continuation. Force the root once; Q_self follows old stochastic actor until H48 and fixed risk tail; Q_ref follows fixed deterministic risk matching through H64. No realized source future enters forecasts.

**Update and compute match.** Center the selected arm's legal Q under old masked policy probabilities, divide by 10, maximize all-action expected advantage under the new policy. Adam3e-4, five deterministic shuffled epochs, minibatch64, full-policy old/new KL penalty1 and rollback/stop if mean KL>0.02, as V10. No entropy bonus, critic network, teacher labels or actor capacity change. Both arms compute all Q_self+Q_ref roots (two continuations × four scenarios) on their own on-policy states, so simulator cost and information access are matched; first-iteration states/Q banks must be byte-equivalent. Log both banks per state, scenario/transition counts, source/root regret, update count, KL/entropy, actor weights/checkpoint hashes and wall/CPU compute. Hard full timeout 3h; no adaptive training extension.

**DEV selection/evaluation.** Evaluate all seven checkpoints per arm/seed on all four cases×16 new DEV physical seeds. Select by four-case mean H+tail cost, tie to earlier iteration, then hash selection. Evaluate both selected V11 arms, immutable five-seed V7 RL comparators and FCFS/risk/rollout on the same DEV seeds. Equal-weight case mean then training/physical seeds. Primary paired contrast is reference minus self cost, with 20,000 hierarchical bootstrap draws over five training seeds and 16 physical seeds shared across cases; report each seed and case, 95% interval, win counts. Comparator training seeds are different and not paired by seed identity. Include H availability/failures/queued CM/PM, starts/pending, cost components, invalid assignments, request conservation, decision time, training and forecast transitions, wall time and failure/timeout state.

**Prelocked DEV gate.** Advance to one separately prelocked fresh TEST and independent method replication only if reference credit improves case-balanced mean cost by ≥10 versus self credit, wins ≥4/5 training seeds including at least three cheapest-control seeds, is ≤1.02× bounded rollout DEV cost with ≥10× faster mean selection, and risk matching does not dominate it in both cost and speed. This gate is exploratory; final goal remains criterion A ≥5% cost below strongest locked baseline or criterion B ≤1% planner cost plus ≥10× speed without simple-heuristic domination, supported by five-seed uncertainty, independent replication, fresh TEST and full reporting. If gate fails, preserve raw negative evidence and inspect scenario variance, continuation bias and case failures; do not open TEST or tune the same panel indefinitely.

Independent verifier replays physical/source-policy stream, sample full Q_self/Q_ref banks, grids, selected episodes, selection/bootstrap and gate before compact report/state/journal are pushed. No SSH, rsync, resets or changes to pre-existing user work.
