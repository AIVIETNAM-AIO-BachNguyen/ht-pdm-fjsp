The two cases were fixed because rollout beat V10 assignment credit by 73.46 and 91.42 episode cost. Each case has five selected V10 training seeds ×16 physical seeds = 80 same-tape pairs; rollout is independently replayed once per physical seed and checked against the locked V10 comparator artifact. The first differing tick is located before outcomes are compared. The root probe forces either policy's first divergent action, then applies **the same deterministic risk-matching continuation** through H64 under 16 new common physical scenarios. Positive ΔQ means the V10 root costs more than the rollout root. This is not the value of following the full rollout policy after the first action.

The prelocked long-horizon timing hypothesis is supported there. In interchangeable the first class is split between machine priority and service timing, so a pure WAIT-only method would miss a large share.

Actual same-tape outcomes remain consistent with material downstream effect: V10 has **+3.225** H failures in interchangeable and **+2.837** in reciprocal2, with failure-cost differences **+72.56/+71.62**; reciprocal2 also has +16.80 unavailability cost and 1.36 fewer service starts. In interchangeable, V10 and rollout have equal mean full-episode service starts, yet the earlier assignment/timing sequence changes later failures. A root-score sign is not by itself proof of actual episode causality: the Q bank is sampled, uses risk continuation, and cannot attribute the entire closed-loop cost gap to a single action. Some individual pairs have a full-horizon Q sign favoring V10 even though rollout's complete policy wins.

## Mechanism decision and next method

The two diagnostics together show a **horizon/credit mismatch**. V10's six-tick on-policy root values were almost tied in the target cases, but a new 16-scenario H64 evaluation of the *first divergent actions* assigns material long-term cost to many early V10 choices. This supports a method that exposes long-term resource and failure consequences at the RL policy update, while preserving the actor's authority over WAIT, machine set and technician assignment. It does not justify a hard WAIT gate, shortest assignment mask, or copying rollout actions.

Next prelock a scratch-RL V11 method with **paired long-horizon continuation-robust counterfactual credit**: the same current-state legal matching actor receives a matched Q bank under its own continuation and a simple risk-policy continuation; the design term uses the latter to represent delayed cost of a root even when the current actor would undo a strategic delay. The ablation gets the exact same Q banks and compute but uses only own-policy Q. Hold actor capacity, case mix, objective, seed splits, checkpoint rule and comparator panel fixed. The risk-policy continuation is a *training critic/reference*, not the dispatcher at inference or a planner teacher label; actor still learns full matching decisions by RL from physical return. Test at least five fresh seeds, uncertainty, strong baselines, and full operational/compute reporting. This is a hypothesis, not a claimed novelty or success. If it fails, analyze forecast variance and continuation bias before another algorithm change. Goal remains **ACTIVE**; no V10 TEST or independent replication.
