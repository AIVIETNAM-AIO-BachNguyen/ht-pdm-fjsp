# V15 release-hazard feature probe — prelocked V14 training Q bank

Locked before implementing or reading any new V15 feature/Q probe result. This is an **exploratory representation/value check**, not V15 training, independent evaluation or TEST. It uses only the completed and independently audited V14 `count_calibrated` training states and their already saved old-policy H64 common-scenario root Q banks. It cannot establish a new policy's closed-loop improvement. The V14 identity DEV result and V15 proposal are known; no fresh physical seed is opened.

## Question and exact current-information features

For each current state, legal matching and fixed V14 count actor checkpoint, compute the V15 proposal's incremental `F_Z(a)=Σ_n w_n(s)[Z_n(a)-Z_n(WAIT)]`, where `Z_n(a)=1-(1-p)^max(0,D_n(a)-max(0,failure_age-age_n-1))` for unfailed, unserviced, unstarted machines and zero for machines started by a. `D_n(a)` is the earliest compatible technician release under the action. Compute the matched age-agnostic control `F_B(a)=Σ_n w_n(s)[B_n(a)-B_n(WAIT)]`, `B_n(a)=1-(1-p)^D_n(a)` for the same machines, with zero when started. Existing service commitments contribute their residual time. `w_n(s)` is the mean existing V14 nonnegative demand output over compatible technicians. WAIT has zero incremental feature. No future shock, source Q value or planner result enters feature computation.

Reconstruct every pre-update count actor checkpoint, observation, legal set and saved Q bank for five V14 training seeds 72600000–04 × six updates × four cases × 64 ranked states = 7,680 states. Score each legal action by old count actor logit minus `alpha*F_Z` or `alpha*F_B`; use the registered catalogue tie rule. No action mask change. Train no new network. For each of five leave-one-training-seed-out folds, select alpha separately for Z and B from **[0,0.25,0.5,1,2,4,8,16,32]** by the lowest mean saved Q of selected actions on the other four seeds, tie to smaller alpha. Evaluate both fitted choices once on the held-out seed's 1,536 rows. No case-specific alpha or post-result grid extension.

## Locked metric, gate and caveats

Primary paired estimand: held-out mean saved Q `Q(a_Z)-Q(a_B)` over the full five-seed/four-case/update-balanced grid. Negative favors the age-threshold release feature. Hierarchical percentile CI95: 20,000 draws with seed **72640003**, resample five held-out training seeds, then 64 state ranks independently within each of the six-update/four-case cells for each selected seed. A *go-to-implementation diagnostic signal* requires mean ≤−1 Q cost unit/state, negative result in at least 4/5 held-out seeds, CI upper <0, and Z/B chosen actions differing on ≥1% of held-out states. This is deliberately only a method-screening gate; a pass is not a learned RL ablation or success on the user's goal. If the gate fails, do not launch a V15 scratch-RL campaign based solely on this feature; explain whether the feature is inactive, indistinguishable from the control or misaligned with old-policy Q.

Secondary: selected alpha by fold/proxy; baseline alpha0 Q; changed-action fraction, service-cardinality/machine-set/assignment/WAIT classes; per-case and per-update Q differences; per-seed Q; feature range across legal matchings; fraction of states with material feature difference (range≥0.05); Z/B feature-ranking discordance; positive/negative/tied Q effects and conditional changed-action Q. Count near-tie logits explicitly. Report existing bank scenario count and old-actor continuation limitation. Do not infer causal closed-loop gains or novelty from same-bank offline selection.
