# V17 conditional method proposal — full-matching relative-Q policy projection

Status: **proposal before any V17 code, seed opening, training or TEST**. Original goal success criteria remain unchanged. A fresh scratch-RL campaign is warranted only if this formulation and its equal-capacity ablation are prospectively locked and run with independent audit.

## 1. Concrete problem and evidence

V14's 3,219-parameter count control already uses all-action H64 counterfactual Q, but updates its current policy by expected Q advantage with a tight KL bound. The audited V14 identity ablation had no robust gain, and V15/V16 explicit release additions were too small under their locked gates. The retrospective, cross-training-seed V16 diagnostic found that a 9,025-parameter full-matching value scorer using only **pre-decision** release information selected actions with saved old-policy Q 3.587 lower than V14's *deterministic* count actor (CI95 [−4.684,−2.507]), negative in all five training seeds and four cases. That comparison had known aggregate baseline, four-scenario old-policy Q and no closed-loop evaluation; it shows headroom for learning action rankings, not policy improvement. V10 first-divergence H64 analysis also found repeated timing choices matter more than a six-tick root view. The testable bottleneck is therefore how the policy projects the complete legal matching Q ranking into its next policy under scarce data and nonpreemptive commitments, not an additional release scalar.

## 2. Idea, formulation and operation

Use a shared full-matching scorer with 56 current-state features, 8 incidence flags, 4 served flags and 6 pre-decision release channels, followed by a 64–64 MLP and one score per legal matching (9,025 parameters). Legal action mask remains exact, WAIT retained, and all features use current information only. Policy is masked softmax of negative predicted cost during on-policy training and argmin predicted cost for deterministic evaluation. Starting from random weights, collect own-policy trajectories; on selected states estimate full-H64 root Q for **every legal matching** with the same common four future-scenario tapes and the *old* policy as continuation. In each state, build a relative target distribution `t(a|s) ∝ exp[−(Q_old(s,a)−mean_legal Q_old)/10]`. Minimize full-legal-action cross-entropy `−Σ t log π_new` plus the existing equal-weight within-served-set assignment credit and old-policy KL trust penalty, rejecting an update if whole-batch `KL(π_old||π_new)>0.02`. Iterate collection and projection for a fixed budget. The centered Q cancels large state offsets; all legal matching alternatives receive explicit credit even if the old policy assigned them little probability. No planner/imitation/pretraining or online future tape enters deployment.

## 3. Why this fits and fair ablation

One technician service can remove capacity for several ticks; action cardinality, machine identity and worker assignment interact. Full-matching Q ranking exposes these consequences jointly. The control has the **identical 9,025-parameter actor, observations, legal mask, current release channels, Q-bank scenario count, root count, optimizer steps, assignment auxiliary and KL bound**, but uses V14's expected-Q advantage objective rather than the relative listwise target. Thus a positive ablation isolates the projection/credit objective under same capacity and nominal compute. Both arms collect their own on-policy states; exact Q banks diverge and this is disclosed. V14 original count/joint, flat PPO, V7 structured policies, FCFS, risk matching and bounded rollout remain strong external comparators. Measure actual training/forecast/inference time and memory; an equal root count alone is not compute equality.

## 4. Prior work and narrow possible contribution

[Learning to Dispatch](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/file/11958dfee29b6709f48a9ba0387a2431-Paper.pdf) already learns structured dispatch rules from scratch. [AirME](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/19839) already frames stochastic maintenance resource choices with RL. [Value Function Based Production Scheduling](https://publications.ri.cmu.edu/value-function-based-production-scheduling) and established policy-iteration / KL projection methods cover value-guided scheduling and listwise policy updates in general. No claim that ranking loss, Q learning, full-matching enumeration or KL control is invented here. A defensible *application-specific method* contribution would require showing that exact legal full-matching relative-Q projection addresses shared-maintenance commitment competition, with a same-architecture ablation and competitive performance. A targeted primary-literature review must expand before any publication-level novelty statement.

## 5. Falsifiable hypothesis and risks

Primary: after five fresh independent scratch-RL training seeds and fixed selection/final DEV splits, ranking projection lowers equal-case H64 economic cost versus the same-actor expected-Q control with seed-consistent uncertainty and approaches the bounded rollout/strongest comparator without WAIT collapse or heuristic dominance. The old-bank Q gain may disappear because greedy changes future state distribution, Q has four-scenario noise, or the projection yields too many one-step improvements that damage long-horizon outcomes. Rank projection may be a generic optimization change rather than a research-level new method; a positive ablation alone cannot establish the user's goal.

## 6. Ablations, decisions and continuation

Prelock full campaign seeds, six-update budget, root/episode quotas, fixed optimizer and checkpoint selection, cost aggregation, uncertainty, baseline versions, audit schema and stopping rule before implementation. Require focused tests, Ubuntu smoke, source push, timestamped full artifacts, tqdm and hard timeout. If DEV passes its material/uncertainty/competitiveness gate, freeze the exact method and run an independent five-seed replication on disjoint training/physical seeds before one fresh frozen TEST. If it fails, report mechanism/per-case/seed evidence, preserve negative run and pursue a distinct hypothesis rather than tuning on TEST. Do not use V16 post-release channels as the claimed new ingredient; their controlled gate failed.
