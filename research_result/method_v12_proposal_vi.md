# V12 proposal: uncertainty-calibrated self-continuation action credit

This is a proposed learning mechanism, not a novelty or performance claim. The decision state and constrained matching action are unchanged from V10: threshold-admitted PM/CM requests, shared technicians, legal technician–machine matching or WAIT, and cost through H64 including terminal tail. The 1,597-parameter shared-edge actor remains fully free over legal matchings. Earlier V10 already used H64 old-actor continuation and conditional same-machine-set assignment credit, but its two-scenario Q estimate had a weak-seed-rescue ablation and remained above the bounded planner. V11 showed that risk-follow-up Q can shift the initial WAIT-logit gradient toward WAIT in all five matched seeds; its method failed. The new hypothesis is that per-state, per-action counterfactual Q noise also weakens robust credit under old-actor continuation.

For a selected on-policy state, simulate each legal root under the same four scenario tapes and old actor through H48 plus fixed risk tail H64. Let `C[s,a]` be scenario cost and `p[a]` the old masked actor distribution. The paired scenario residual is `D[s,a] = C[s,a] − Σ_b p[b] C[s,b]`; its mean is the ordinary expected-cost action advantage (up to sign). Estimate standard error across the four paired scenarios. The new arm uses `A[a] = −mean(D[:,a])/10 × min(1, |mean(D[:,a])|/(2 SE(D[:,a]) + 1e−6))`. The ablation uses the same Q bank, same actor/objective and forecast compute with multiplier 1. Both retain V10's λ=0.5 conditional assignment improvement and KL-regularized actor update. This attenuation is a fixed reliability rule, not a hard service mask; legal actions and actor representation remain identical. Its possible weakness is shrinking useful rare-tail differences. Four scenarios only give a noisy standard error; report action-weight distributions and failures.

Primary test: five fresh independent train seeds, matched initialization and iteration-1 physical tape, paired DEV seeds; candidate versus plain H64 self-credit with both using four scenarios, 64 selected states/case/update, six updates, same optimizer/checkpoint selection. Comparator panel includes V7 flat/shared/canonical PPO, FCFS, risk matching and bounded rollout. Require ablation improvement in at least four of five seeds including strong controls, case-balanced cost near the planner, selection speed ≥10×, and no heuristic dominating both. V12 remains DEV-only until prelocked promotion. A passing DEV result would need a fresh locked TEST and independent replication before goal completion. Related-work distinction must stay modest: this is a paired-scenario confidence weighting of action credit in the maintenance matching setting, potentially overlapping known variance reduction and robust policy optimization ideas; it is not a generic RL invention.
