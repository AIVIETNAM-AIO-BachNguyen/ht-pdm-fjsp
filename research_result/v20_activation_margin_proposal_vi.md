# V20 prospective method proposal — counterfactual matching activation margin

**Problem and evidence.** Frozen V14 joint cost 858.23 versus rollout 843.38 on the fresh V19 DEV panel, with +60.14/+70.88 in interchangeable/reciprocal2. Direct common-shock first-root Q says the V14 root is worse by +35.16 [+25.11,+45.59] and +59.90 [+46.87,+73.11] under its own greedy continuation; V14 serves when rollout waits at 32/80 and 45/80 first divergences. Six-tick Q is near zero but H64 effects are large. Retrospective V14 training banks include early states where WAIT Q is ≥5 better yet selected joint V14 serves in 92/92 and 114/168. The separate V14 count-calibrated actor has slightly lower total cost (857.84) and V14 identity-specific ablation failed. The mechanism to test is **whole-matching start/WAIT calibration**, not machine identity or generic future-capacity features.

**Formulation.** Retain the V14 count-calibrated actor's current 56-observation, full legal joint-matchings, 3,219 active parameters and shared-resource demand scoring. Define service logits for all non-WAIT legal matchings and one WAIT logit. Factor the deployed policy into a binary activation probability and a conditional matching distribution: π(WAIT|s)=1−g(s), π(a|s)=g(s)·π(a|start,s). Both V20 arms use the *same* count-normalized log-sum-exp activation map and conditional softmax, so action multiplicity, architecture, information and inference cost are controlled. The candidate trains g from the counterfactual margin Q(WAIT)−min_nonWAIT Q(a), with a fixed soft target and bounded margin weight, while training conditional matching among service actions from the same Q bank. The control trains the same factorized policy with a generic expected-Q improvement objective over all legal actions. Both are scratch RL on their own states; Q bank uses four common stochastic-failure scenarios, forcing each root and then the old policy's **greedy deployed action** through H48 plus unchanged risk tail to H64. Neither policy receives future shocks or an online planner at inference. No heuristic action restriction is imposed.

**Why this may fit.** A maintenance start occupies a technician for several ticks and can block later corrective/PM requests. WAIT defers the entire start set for one tick, after which the policy observes again. V14's single flat expected-Q update mixes the WAIT decision with 20 possible matchings and did not reliably follow its own WAIT-favoring Q examples. Candidate makes the cost of committing shared technicians a direct training target while retaining V14's conditional matching structure and global current observation. Greedy continuation aligns the Q question with the deployed policy, but is identical in both arms; the ablation isolates activation-margin learning.

**Boundary against prior work.** Learning to Dispatch already learns RL scheduling rules; AirME/HetGPO already learns stochastic maintenance timing with shared crews; Option-Critic and other hierarchical RL already separate decisions; learned matching systems already retain resources for future demand. See `v20_primary_prior_art_boundary_vi.md`. We claim no generic novelty for hierarchy, gating, waiting or resource reservation. The testable scope is a counterfactual whole-matching activation margin under persistent threshold requests and joint non-preemptive technician allocation, with equal-capacity/same-bank control. Any originality claim remains conditional on fresh results and deeper search.

**Falsifiable hypotheses and controls.** H1: candidate minus control four-case H64 economic cost ≤−10, with five-seed hierarchical CI95 upper<0, at least four of five seed means improving including all three strongest control seeds. H2: candidate ≤1.01× bounded rollout, ≥10× faster selection and not jointly dominated by simple FCFS/risk; candidate must also beat frozen V14 count on the fresh panel. Track WAIT frequency, service starts, H failures, PM/CM queues and per-case cost; a gain only in the two weak cases that destroys reciprocal3 is failure. Frozen V14 joint/count, V7 flat PPO, FCFS, risk and bounded rollout are required comparators. The generic expected-Q arm is the same-capacity/core-component ablation. The original goal's alternative 5% improvement over strongest comparator remains separately evaluated.

**Decision rule.** This is exploratory five-seed DEV only. A positive H1/H2 can motivate one independent replication and then one frozen fresh TEST; it does not complete the goal. A negative result retires this mechanism without tuning on TEST. Full implementation/protocol/seed registry, focused tests and Ubuntu smoke with independent audit precede source push and full run. No source, physics, objective or guardrail changes after viewing evaluation.
