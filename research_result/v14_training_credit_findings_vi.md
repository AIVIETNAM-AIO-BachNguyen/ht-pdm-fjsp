# V14 logged training Q versus selected service-start decisions

**Status:** retrospective opened-training probe COMPLETE and independent audit PASS. Plan `v14_training_credit_probe_plan_vi.md` was pushed before inspecting Q/logits. No new training, physical episodes or TEST.

At early ticks <16, both WAIT and service were legal in **660 logged states per case** (132 per training seed). Define ΔQ = best-service Q − WAIT Q; positive means WAIT is better in the historical four-scenario stochastic old-policy forecast bank. The frozen *selected* actor is evaluated on each logged state, not the actor that generated the Q label.

| Case, early states | ΔQ≥5 (WAIT better) | Selected actor serves despite ΔQ≥5 | Selected actor WAIT overall | Selected actor Q regret≥5 | Mean selected regret |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Interchangeable | 92/660 (13.94%) | **92/92 (100%)** | **0/660** | 35.30% | 7.21 |
| Reciprocal2 | 168/660 (25.45%) | **114/168 (67.86%)** | 20.15% | 57.88% | 24.07 |

Every V14 training seed shows this phenomenon: interchangeable has 12–22 early WAIT-favored-by-≥5 rows per seed and selects service in every one; reciprocal2 has 31–37 such rows per seed, with 19–26 still selecting service. Across all H48 ticks, selected WAIT is 0% in interchangeable and 22.81% in reciprocal2. The historical Q bank itself more often favors service: early mean ΔQ −16.79/−26.45. Thus the evidence supports a **calibration/optimization failure** on some states with informative Q labels and leaves a separate **credit/continuation bias** possible on other states. The direct V14-vs-rollout diagnostic evaluated different first-divergence states with a greedy continuation and found large positive service-root penalties; this probe does not align individual states or Q definitions across the two datasets.

**Decision.** A next V14-anchored method should explicitly learn the WAIT-versus-start contrast and conditional matching separately, with the same information, architecture capacity, sampled states, Q tapes, optimizer budget and strong baselines in its ablation. The method must also preserve the ability to serve promptly in reciprocal3, where V14 already beats rollout. Do not call a separate gate or weighted loss novel by itself; compare primary prior art and make any claim conditional on the actual mechanism and fresh five-seed performance. Neither this retrospective probe nor the earlier direct diagnostic promotes V14. Original A/B, independent replication and fresh frozen TEST remain open.
