# V9 proposal: paired counterfactual credit for an RL matching dispatcher

Written before V9 code, smoke, training or DEV outcomes. This is a proposed method, not a result or novelty claim. V8 DEV evidence: on current-request states, canonical shared PPO lost mostly through choosing among already legal actions (mean actor gap 3.307 versus mask gap 0.047); weak training replicas were responsible for much of that loss. Exact material V7 local-edge representation conflicts covered only 8/720 audited states, and a small supervised local-edge scorer nearly matched the risk rule on heldout bounded-planner root choices. V4/V5 age2 and fixed post-decision capacity penalties failed. Thus changing actor context or appending another hand-set opportunity term alone has poor evidentiary support; the next falsifiable mechanism is **action credit under shared-resource competition**.

## Formulation and mechanism

One centralized learned actor still makes the final H48 dispatch decision. Current threshold admission/request lifecycle, 56-D observation, 21 partial-matching catalogue including WAIT, legal feasibility mask, four N4/K2 cases, H48+tail16 economic objective and physical randomness remain unchanged. The actor has the V7 shared-edge parameters and no planner, forecast or realized future input at inference. No imitation, teacher labels, pretraining, fine-tuning, reward shaping or added economic penalty is used.

At on-policy states collected with the *current stochastic actor*, enumerate every legal matching. For each, sample the same independent common-random-number forecast bank. Force that matching once, then continue with the **same current actor** (not risk, rollout or an optimizing planner) through the remaining H48 ticks; apply the fixed risk tail through H64. Simulated return is the actual unchanged negative H+tail cost from that state onward. This yields paired Monte Carlo estimates Q_old(s,a) for all feasible current actions under the old policy. Center Q within the legal set and update the actor with the action-integrated softmax policy gradient, subject to a small old-to-new KL trust region. At runtime, only one actor forward pass plus the feasibility mask is needed. The core question is whether enumerating coupled matching alternatives and pairing future randomness gives reliable credit for technician commitments that on-trajectory PPO advantages miss.

The clean ablation has the **same actor, on-policy states, exact forecast bank, Q estimates, training/evaluation budget and optimizer** but updates only from the action actually sampled on the source trajectory (with an action-independent baseline from the same bank). This controls extra simulation compute and separates all-action credit integration from mere access to a model. Both arms are scratch RL. Separate V7 structured and flat PPO checkpoints, risk matching and bounded rollout are context baselines on the same DEV physical seeds; comparisons to historical PPO training compute are reported rather than falsely equated.

## Prior work and defensible distinction

[Expected Policy Gradients, JMLR 2020](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume21/18-012/18-012.pdf) explicitly sums over discrete actions; [Discrete Action On-Policy Learning with Action-Value Critic, AISTATS 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/yue20a.html) likewise addresses action-value credit in discrete RL. COMA's counterfactual baseline is established in MARL. [Learning to Dispatch, NeurIPS 2020](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/file/11958dfee29b6709f48a9ba0387a2431-Paper.pdf) learns graph-based dispatching; [HetGPO/AirME, ICAPS 2022](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/19839) learns shared-crew maintenance scheduling with heterogeneous graphs and robust policy optimization. V9 does **not** claim to invent all-action gradients, counterfactual baselines, model-based rollouts, shared-crew RL or global maintenance decisions. Its specific design distinction is full H+tail paired, current-actor continuation for all feasible *joint* technician matchings in the validated persistent-request simulator, used as a training-only credit estimator and isolated against a matched chosen-action update. Whether this is a publishable contribution depends on ablation magnitude, strong baselines, cost/runtime and independent replication.

## Falsifiable hypotheses and limits

H1: on new DEV physical seeds, all-action credit reduces case-balanced economic cost relative to compute-matched chosen-action credit across the five independent training seeds, not solely by rescuing one or two weak replicas. H2: gains correspond to fewer high-regret WAIT, machine-set or technician-assignment choices on same-state common-bank diagnostics, and improve failures/availability/CM queue without hiding cost shifts. H3: if competitive, learned inference is ≥10 times faster than bounded rollout and is not dominated by the simple risk rule. If H1 fails or the actor still lags risk/rollout, the all-action estimator is not a successful method merely because it reduces gradient variance in theory. Finite Monte Carlo bank, policy-induced distribution shift, full-horizon forecast cost and actor representation remain limitations. Do not claim size or permutation generalization without a separate test.
