# V3 post hoc action diagnostic plan

Written after the v3 sealed panel was opened and the independent audit passed.
This is exploratory mechanism analysis, not a new confirmation test or a change
to the registered H1/H2 verdict. Preserve the original run and selection.

Question: how do scratch PPO, warm imitation, and teacher PPO differ in their
legal dispatch decisions and queue exposure across the four fixed cases?
Trace each DEV-selected checkpoint on the already opened 64 TEST physical
seeds per case, with the existing action diagnostic. Record WAIT while a
service is legal, nonmaximal matching, PM/CM starts, slow eligible edges,
agreement with risk matching, queued CM exposure, availability, and cost.
Trajectories diverge after actions, so trace comparisons are descriptive and
cannot isolate a causal decision on a common state. No new selection or
training. Three new timestamped directories, one per controller; stop after
256 episodes per checkpoint. Use the diagnostic's source/checkpoint hashes.
