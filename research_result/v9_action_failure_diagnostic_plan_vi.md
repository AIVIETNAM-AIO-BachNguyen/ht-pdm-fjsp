# Prelocked V9 selected-policy action failure diagnostic (DEV only)

Locked 2026-10-20 **after** the independently audited V9 DEV result and **before** this diagnostic's implementation/outcomes. It is exploratory mechanism analysis on already-opened V9 development physical seeds, not a new method, TEST, replication or fresh confirmation. V9 all-action cost was 961.27 versus risk 863.72 and rollout 857.81; reciprocal cases had fewer service starts and much more corrective queue time. The aggregates do not establish which dispatch decisions caused this pattern. Previous V8 local-edge/context and V5 post-decision feature negatives remain in force.

**Question/hypotheses.** On the exact selected V9 deterministic trajectories, do weak V9 seeds 02–03 (and any strong seeds) WAIT or dispatch fewer requests when a current legal service is available, choose the wrong machine subset, or choose inferior technician assignments? Does a bounded current-information planner assign a material six-tick value gap to those choices? H1: under-service timing/cardinality dominates reciprocal failures and is more prevalent for weak than strong V9 seeds. H2 alternative: machine priority/assignment, not WAIT, dominates. H3 alternative: bounded root values are too small or inconsistent to explain full-episode cost; then investigate long-horizon/continuation effects. This analysis cannot causally infer full-episode gain from root regret.

**Population and trace.** Use immutable selected checkpoints and selection hashes from `framework_v9_dev_20261020T062502Z`. Reproduce complete deterministic H48+tail16 episodes for all four cases × both V9 arms × five training seeds × fixed V9 DEV physical seeds 70310000–15 (640 episodes; 30,720 H-tick decisions). Independently check each episode's objective, cost components, H availability/failures, request conservation and zero invalid assignments against V9 saved `episodes.csv`. At each pre-action state record present threshold-admitted legal matchings, selected matching, risk matching, queued PM/CM, available technicians, selected/risk cardinality and machine sets, selected/risk service duration, and a mutually exclusive action class: match, WAIT while risk serves, service while risk WAITs, lower/higher service cardinality, same cardinality/different machine subset, same machine subset/different technician. Separately count WAIT with *any* nonempty legal matching and waits when no service is legal. Stratify by case, arm, training seed and clock bin 0–15/16–31/32–47; report H service starts and corrective queue exposure from the episode grid. Do not equate disagreement with risk to an error.

**Bounded root-value check.** Among pre-action states with ≥2 legal actions, hash-rank `(version, case, arm, train seed, physical seed, tick)` within each case×arm×training-seed×clock-bin stratum, keep first **2**. Maximum 240 states; do not replace sparse strata. For each selected original state, score *every* legal root with the existing V7 bounded current-information rollout: 16 new independent forecast scenarios, horizon6, threshold submissions and risk-continuation; one common bank across roots, deterministic baseline-first tie. Forecast anchor **70360000**, disjoint from V9 training bank 70320000. Smoke uses 1 scenario and at most 1 selected state per stratum from the first fixed DEV physical seed only. Record source, same-seed other V9 arm, risk and bounded planner choices on each state, root scores, regret differences and taxonomy/cardinality. Report mean/sums/counts with gaps ≥1/5/10 by strong seeds 00/01/04 versus weak 02/03, case and clock bin. This is a forecast-root diagnostic, not economic cost or true optimality.

**Decision gate.** If weak-seed reciprocal failure has materially frequent WAIT/lower cardinality with same-state forecast ≥5 cost gap, formulate a learned timing/service-commitment component that can be ablated without heuristic action restriction; compare it with risk and strong seeds. If a different machine subset/assignment dominates, target that structure instead. If bounded root margins are small, test longer credit/continuation before changing actor. No V10 architecture is selected by one summary count alone. A future method still needs the unchanged full goal completion bar and fresh TEST only after prelock.
