# Prelocked V20 counterfactual activation-margin scratch-RL DEV protocol

Locked before V20 policy/campaign implementation or any V20 RL outcome. Proposal `v20_activation_margin_proposal_vi.md` and primary-literature boundary `v20_primary_prior_art_boundary_vi.md` define the question. This is one new DEV campaign after multiple prior negative/positive explorations, not a confirmatory TEST.

## Fixed setting, arms and information

Unchanged four V1 cases at equal 1/4 weight, threshold requester, full 21 joint matching catalogue including WAIT, H48 policy plus H16 immutable risk tail, simulator physics and economic objective. Lower H64 cost is better. Two scratch-RL arms: `activation_margin` and `generic_q`. Both use V14 count-calibrated 56-current-observation actor, identical 3,219 active parameters/initial weights per paired seed, identical legal actions, count-normalized log-sum-exp binary activation map and conditional service softmax at training **and inference**. Both collect own-policy states and compute every legal root under four common failure scenarios with old-actor **greedy deployed continuation** to H48 and risk tail to H64. Forecast/uniform shocks are never actor inputs.

For legal service set S(s), raw actor logits z, B(s)=logsumexp(z_a, a∈S)−log|S|, g=σ(B−z_WAIT), π(WAIT)=1−g, π(a∈S)=g·softmax(z_a over S). When S is empty, WAIT probability is one. Deployed greedy action is argmax of this factorized distribution, with catalogue-index tie break. No action is prohibited beyond simulator legality.

At each training state let q be the four-scenario mean H64 cost of each legal root; q_best=min_a∈S q_a, margin=q_best−q_WAIT. Candidate gate target t_WAIT=σ(margin/10), weight w=min(2,|margin|/10). Its gate loss is w times binary cross entropy between t_WAIT and π(WAIT). Both arms have conditional matching gain 0.5·sum_a∈S π(a|start)·(mean_S q−q_a)/10. Generic control's global gain is sum_a π(a)·(sum_b π_old(b)q_b−q_a)/10, with old-policy target detached. Candidate objective is gate loss minus conditional gain plus old→new KL; generic objective is minus global gain minus the *same* conditional gain plus the same KL. Gradients come from each arm's own full-root Q bank; no imitation, pretraining or planner labels. A clipping/smoothing change after DEV scores is prohibited.
