## Arms, fixed physics and main metric

Four unchanged V1 cases, threshold requester, H48+16 risk tail, same economic objective/physics, all legal partial matchings including WAIT. Both arms use identical 9,025-parameter V17 full-matching actor, 74 current-information inputs and same initial weights per paired training seed. For each selected on-policy state, compute every legal root on four common Bernoulli future scenarios under **both** old actor continuation and deterministic risk-matching continuation, with same failure tapes. Control `own_rank` uses the exact V17 rank target `softmax(-Q_own/10)`. Candidate `bounded_disagreement` uses `Q_own+0.5*clip[(Q_risk-mean_legal Q_risk)-(Q_own-mean_legal Q_own),−40,+40]`, then `softmax(-Q_bounded/10)`. Both use assignment auxiliary from Q_own only (λ0.5), old-policy KL penalty1/hard whole-bank KL≤0.02, Adam3e−4, five epochs, minibatch64, gradient clip0.5, and the same batch mean service-flow penalty `10*ReLU(E_old[|a|]−E_new[|a|]−0.05)^2`. Zero weight decay. Candidate and control compute both Q banks and receive no future tape at deployment. Report nonzero clipped corrections and WAIT gradient/probability changes; do not treat the soft service-flow term as an action feasibility rule.

Primary final-DEV estimand: paired equal-four-case H64 cost `bounded_disagreement−own_rank` over five training seeds ×16 shared final physical seeds, with 20,000 hierarchical percentile draws/bootstrap seed73340000 resampling training seeds, then physical seeds **within each selected training seed**. Require mean ≤−10, CI upper<0, negative per-seed mean in ≥4/5 seeds including ≥2/3 strongest-control seeds. Promotion additionally requires candidate ≤1.02× bounded rollout, ≥10× faster selection, **candidate cost no higher than frozen V14 joint** on this same physical panel, and no risk-matching cost/runtime dominance. Must report service starts, legal-service WAIT fraction, CM/PM queues, H failures/availability and cost parts to detect V11-style collapse. This DEV gate is not itself full user-goal completion; original ≤1% planner or ≥5% strongest comparator plus independent replication/fresh TEST remain required.
