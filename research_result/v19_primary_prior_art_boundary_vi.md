# V19 primary-literature boundary — checked during running DEV

This note does **not** modify V19's locked method, simulator, comparator, seed split, gate or source hashes. It is a targeted, non-exhaustive prior-work check, not a claim of novelty or V19 efficacy. The full five-seed V19 DEV run was still training when this note was written.

- [Zhang and Dietterich, *High-Performance Job-Shop Scheduling With A Time-Delay TD(λ) Network*, NeurIPS 1995](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/1995/file/4f16c818875d9fcb6867c7bdc89be7eb-Paper.pdf) already use a **time-indexed resource utilization** measure and TD learning for scheduling. V19 cannot claim that exposing future resource occupancy to RL is new.
- [Tassel et al., *Reinforcement Learning of Dispatching Strategies for Large-Scale Industrial Scheduling*, ICAPS 2022](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/download/19852/19611/23865) learn RL dispatching strategies for resource-constrained projects whose resource capacities vary by time and tasks are non-preemptive. V19 cannot claim that learning a dispatch policy under time-varying capacities or non-preemptive commitments is new.
- [Panda, Xiang and Liu, *Dynamic resource matching in manufacturing using deep reinforcement learning*, EJOR 2024 / author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.27066) formulate multi-period matching with random demand, backlog and limited capacities as an RL problem. They add prior-policy and infeasibility penalties. V19 cannot claim that dynamic demand-capacity matching, backlog, or domain-informed RL are new. Their paper's problem and algorithm differ from V19's present-state nine-offset maximum-feasible-matching deficit as an action feature, but this check alone does not establish the latter's novelty.
- [Wang and Gombolay, *Stochastic Resource Optimization over Heterogeneous Graph Neural Networks for Failure-Predictive Maintenance Scheduling*, ICAPS 2022](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/19839) already address stochastic maintenance scheduling with a global graph policy and shared repair resources, as documented in `v18_primary_prior_art_review_vi.md`.

V19's potentially defensible scope, **conditional on a positive fresh matched ablation, strong comparator performance, independent replication and deeper literature search**, is a particular action-dependent temporal *maximum compatible matching shortfall* computed from persistent-request thresholds and technician release times, used as current-policy input. We must not describe the time profile, matching, resource awareness or maintenance RL separately as a new invention. A failed ablation or performance gate would eliminate even this empirical method claim.
