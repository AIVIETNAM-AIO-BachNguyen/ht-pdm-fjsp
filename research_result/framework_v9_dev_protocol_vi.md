# V9 scratch-RL paired-counterfactual DEV protocol

Locked 2026-10-20 before V9 implementation/outcomes. Proposal: `method_v9_proposal_vi.md`. **Exploratory DEV campaign only**; no opened V7/V8 TEST reuse. Source model, current-information contract, four cases, threshold requester, H48+tail16 reward/cost, legal-action feasibility and tail risk are version-frozen from V7. Learned actor is exact V7 `EdgeSharingActor('shared_edge')` with **free legal mask** in both V9 arms. Flat/free and V7 shared free/canonical selected checkpoints are read-only comparator references on the new DEV panel; no changed V7 weights. Strong rules: FCFS, risk matching and V7 bounded rollout (16 scenarios, horizon6, risk continuation). Feasibility is provided by mask, never attributed to learning.

## Seeds, training and stopping

Five independent actor/training seeds 70300000–04; paired initial actor weights per seed across `all_action` and `chosen_action`. Training physical episode seeds and forecast-bank seeds are derived by `role_seed` from `(version, train_seed, arm-independent iteration, case, episode or sampled-state index, scenario)`; streams for environment and forecast are disjoint. New DEV physical seeds 70310000–15 per case; smoke 70390000–01; forecast anchor 70320000; bootstrap 70340000. Check registry against earlier numerical seed ranges. Six fixed policy-improvement iterations, plus initial checkpoint. At each iteration and arm: 16 stochastic on-policy source episodes per case (64 total, H48 each); hash-select up to 64 multi-action states per case (256 total), balanced across tick bins 0–15,16–31,32–47 by fixed quotas. If a bin lacks enough multi-action states, use all available and report shortfall, no outcome-driven replacement. Compute Q_old(s,a) for **every legal root** with 2 independent common physical/policy-random scenarios, forced root action then current stochastic actor through H48 and fixed risk tail to H64. Forecast randomness is independent of the realized source trajectory and shared across candidate roots. Use undiscounted cost-to-go for the exact episodic objective, not short-horizon planner labels. All-action arm uses every Q; chosen-action arm receives the same full bank but its gradient uses only the trajectory-selected action and a statewise baseline computed from the bank. No candidate planner choice is fed as a teacher label.

Actor update: masked softmax, centered return advantages divided by fixed scale 10, Adam 3e-4, 5 epochs over the selected state set in deterministic minibatches of 64. All-action minimizes minus expected current-policy advantage; chosen-action uses old-policy importance-ratio sampled-action policy gradient, both with identical old-to-new KL penalty coefficient 1.0 and a hard early stop if mean KL exceeds 0.02. No entropy bonus, reward modification or critic. Record objective, KL, entropy, policy update counts, root/scenario/simulator transitions and CPU/wall compute each iteration. Checkpoint actor after every iteration. Use the same training case/physical-seed tape for both arms within each seed; trajectories may diverge after policy updates.

DEV evaluation: every checkpoint 0–6 for 2 arms×5 seeds×4 cases×16 new physical seeds. Select one checkpoint per arm/seed by four-case DEV mean H+tail cost, ties earlier iteration; hash selection before any later confirmation. Evaluate selected V9 checkpoints, immutable V7 selected `flat_free`, `shared_free`, `shared_canonical` checkpoints (all five original training replicas), FCFS/risk/rollout on the same DEV physical seeds. Describe V9 versus compute-matched ablation as the primary DEV mechanism contrast; V9 versus strongest planner/rule/flat/structured as competitive context. Case-balanced aggregate is equal mean over four cases, then training and physical seeds. Paired hierarchical bootstrap 20,000 draws resampling five V9 training seeds and 16 physical seeds jointly across four cases for V9 arm contrast; retain per-training-seed means and win counts. Comparator training seeds are different families and are *not* paired by seed identity; report comparator distributions and case/physical pairing without pretending common training seeds. Include cost components, H availability/failures/queued CM/PM, request conservation, decision time, training and forecast compute, timeout/failure records.

DEV go/no-go: advance to one separately prelocked fresh TEST only if all-action is at least 10 cost units cheaper than chosen-action on case-balanced mean, wins ≥4/5 training seeds, and is within 2% of bounded rollout on this DEV panel while retaining ≥10× selection speed. This is an exploration gate, **not** the unchanged goal completion rule. If it fails, analyze action values, policy KL/entropy, state distribution and costs; do not tune this campaign after seeing results or call it successful. Later completion still requires a clear method/prior distinction, fresh ablation with training-seed uncertainty, criterion A (≥5% below strongest locked baseline) or B (≤1% planner cost plus ≥10× speed and no simple-heuristic domination), ≥5 seeds and independent replication, per-case/failure/compute reporting, and pushed provenance.

## Execution controls and artifact schema

Focused tests plus fresh Ubuntu smoke first. Commit/push verified proposal, protocol, registry, source and tests **before** full DEV. tqdm for episodes, counterfactual states, evaluations. Hard timeout 3 hours full / 10 minutes smoke. Fresh actual UTC timestamped directory under `research_result/framework_v9_{smoke|dev}_.../`; refuse existing directory, never silently overwrite. Manifest has source commit and hashes, exact config/runtime/invocation/status/elapsed, seed registry and stream digests; training trace CSV, selected state/action Q summaries and scenario transition counts, checkpoint hashes and per-iteration training metrics; DEV candidate CSV, frozen selections, paired evaluation CSV and summary with intervals. Raw/checkpoints stay ignored outside Git. Independent verifier should replay random streams, a sample of counterfactual Q trajectories, rewards/cost/request conservation, full grids, selection, result aggregation and artifact hashes. No SSH or rsync. Preserve the three pre-existing user changes and all old artifacts.

**Resolved fixed sampling/order detail before implementation.** The per-case 64-state sample uses 22 states from ticks 0–15, 21 from 16–31 and 21 from 32–47, hash-ranked within each case/bin by `(version, training seed, iteration, case, physical episode seed, tick)`. If a stratum has fewer, retain all and do not fill from another bin. Training minibatches use ascending hash rank, with deterministic seed-derived permutation each epoch. At deterministic evaluation, exact catalogue-order argmax breaks ties; stochastic continuation samples a single shared pre-generated uniform for each `(scenario, tick)` across every candidate root. The common bank uses independently drawn Bernoulli failure events for every `(scenario, tick, machine)` through H64 and independent policy uniforms only for ticks before H48. The source physical event tape is never read by the counterfactual evaluator.
