## Questions, estimands and decision rule

Replay all five frozen V14 selected training seeds 72600000–04 and bounded rollout on the **already-opened V19 final physical panel** 73411000–15, for interchangeable_scale1 and reciprocal_scale2. Verify every source metric against the independently audited V19 source episodes before examining action differences. At every H48 tick store action; for each of 5×16 paired episodes per case, record the first common pre-action state where V14 and rollout diverge, categories (same, V14 service/rollout WAIT, V14 WAIT/rollout service, cardinality, machine set, technician assignment), actual H64 cost and failure/queue/service-start deltas. Record the full action streams and first state, not only a summary.

For each divergent first state use 16 fresh common stochastic failure tapes from role anchor 73670000 (all used diagnostic and historical physical/role seeds checked disjoint). Force the V14 or rollout legal root. Then continue to H48 with either frozen V14 greedy policy (`own`) or deterministic risk matching (`risk`), followed by immutable risk tail to H64. Record first-six-tick and H64 root cost difference V14−rollout, every tape and per-scenario value. These Q values are conditional first-root comparisons, not a causal attribution of full policy gaps. Identical trajectories retain zero first-root Q. Report per-case mean actual and root-Q deltas, 5 seed means, category fractions and 20,000-draw hierarchical CI95 resampling five training seeds and 16 physical tapes within seed (bootstrap seed 73670001). A material first-root disadvantage requires full-Q CI lower>0 and mean≥5; a continuation mismatch requires risk-Q lower>0 and own-Q ≤0. If neither appears, inspect later trajectory differences; do not infer an improvement from aggregate cost alone.

Use fixed H48+16 economic objective and original physics, case-balanced summaries and no tuning on this panel. All five V14 models are frozen by the V14 source selection hashes. Preserve the existing source and V19 diagnostic results as immutable provenance. This study can motivate a **new, distinct V14-anchored scratch-RL proposal** with a matched-capacity ablation and fresh five-seed DEV split, but it cannot promote V14, V19 or a future method by itself.
