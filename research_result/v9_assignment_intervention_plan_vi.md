# Prelocked V9 assignment-only closed-loop intervention (DEV only)

Locked after the independently audited V9 action trace (`bd3f78d`) and before intervention code or outcomes. This is an exploratory causal mechanism check on the already-opened V9 DEV tapes, not a new RL method, TEST, replication or selection gate. The environment, threshold requester, cost, H48+tail16, selected checkpoints and physical seeds remain fixed.

**Question and hypothesis.** How much of weak V9 reciprocal-case episode cost is caused by its longer technician assignments for an actor-chosen machine set? H1: replacing only those assignments with a shortest known-duration feasible assignment on the *same machine set* lowers weak-seed reciprocal cost by at least half the observed weak-versus-strong control gap. H2: little or no gain, or harm from displaced specialist opportunity, means subset choice, temporal credit or continuation explains much of the failure. These are descriptive DEV mechanism thresholds, not confirmation of policy quality.

**Paired design.** Use the five selected training checkpoints of each V9 arm, four locked cases and 16 V9 DEV physical seeds 70310000–15: 640 control/intervention pairs, 1,280 episodes. Reset separate environment instances to the identical physical seed. At every H tick, each trajectory calls its own unchanged selected actor on its current observation and legal mask. Control executes that action. Intervention preserves the actor's WAIT/service decision and exact chosen machine set/cardinality, but picks the legal assignment for that set with minimum sum of *currently known* service durations; break equal-duration ties in favor of the actor action, then catalogue order. No treatment decision reads realized future randomness or the control state. Continue the actor after every modified action. Verify control episode metrics against immutable V9 `episodes.csv` to 1e-8. Tail is the original environment's fixed risk policy. Count actual changed assignments and duration savings, separate from endogenous later subset/timing changes.

**Measures.** Primary: paired episode objective difference treatment minus control, by case/arm/training seed, and equal-weight four-case mean. Secondary: maintenance/unavailability/failure/tail costs, H availability/failures, H corrective/preventive queue exposure, H and full service starts, H/end pending requests, created/completed requests, changed assignments and known duration savings. Report number of ties, zeros, positive and negative paired cost effects, and physical-seed paired uncertainty descriptively; five training seeds are the unit for method stability. Strong all-action seeds 00/01/04 are a preidentified negative control for reciprocal assignment differences; weak seeds are 02/03. Specialist cases are a harm check because the bounded planner sometimes favored nonshortest assignment. No change to earlier baseline/competitive criteria.

**Decision and stopping rule.** Complete the fixed grid once, no adaptive extension, new timestamped artifact, maximum 15 minutes. If at least half the weak-vs-strong reciprocal control gap is recovered and strong-seed cost is not harmed by >5%, treat assignment credit as a supported V10 target; still require an RL mechanism that can choose nonshortest assignments and improve strong seeds/competitive baselines. Otherwise pursue subset/long-horizon credit. Any DEV finding remains exploratory; do not open TEST from this intervention alone.

**Workflow and audit.** Implement focused tests, run a fresh Ubuntu smoke with first DEV physical seed and both arms, commit/push verified protocol/source before full DEV. `tqdm` reports paired episodes. Full artifact contains manifest with commit/source/input/model hashes and runtime/status, `episodes.csv`, `actions.csv` and `summary.json`; paths must be new UTC timestamped directories and raw data stays outside Git. An independent verifier replays both trajectories and checks source controls, treatment rule, episode metrics and summary. Commit/push compact audited report and state/journal. Preserve user changes; no SSH or rsync.
