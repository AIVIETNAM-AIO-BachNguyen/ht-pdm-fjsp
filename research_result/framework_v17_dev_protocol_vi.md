## Hypothesis, arms and primary gate

Both arms deploy the same 9,025-parameter current-information 74→64→64→1 full-matching scorer and exact legal mask. Its inputs are the 56-D V1 observation, 8 matching-incidence flags, 4 started-machine flags and 6 *pre-decision* release features; the failed V16 post-decision increment is excluded. Both start with identical random weights for each paired training seed. Both collect own on-policy H48 trajectories, score every legal root on four common stochastic full-H64 old-policy continuations per selected state, and use identical state/episode/Q/optimizer budgets. Their only designed difference is the update's main credit objective:

- `rank_projection`: cross-entropy from `softmax[−(Q(a)−mean_legal Q)/10]` to the new masked policy over all legal actions.
- `expected_q`: V14-style expected-Q advantage objective `Σ_a π_new(a)(E_{π_old}Q−Q(a))/10`.

Both add λ=0.5 equal-weight conditional assignment gain, old-policy KL penalty1, Adam3e−4, five epochs/minibatch64, gradient clip0.5 and whole-bank KL reject threshold0.02. Exact action support, active parameter count, runtime and forecast transitions must be checked. Actor selection is masked argmax; exploration samples masked softmax. No future event tape or planner at deployment; no imitation or pretraining.

Primary fresh final-DEV estimand: `rank_projection−expected_q` H48+tail16 economic cost, average equally over four cases, 16 final physical seeds and five paired training seeds. 20,000-draw hierarchical percentile CI95 using bootstrap seed 73040000 resamples five training seeds and, within each, 16 shared physical seeds. Require mean ≤−5 cost, CI upper<0, negative mean for ≥4/5 training seeds and ≥2/3 lowest-cost control seeds. Additional DEV go requires candidate cost ≤1.02× bounded rollout, at least 10× faster action selection than rollout, and no risk-matching dominance in both cost and runtime. This DEV gate decides replication/TEST readiness; it does not by itself satisfy the user's ≥5% strongest-comparator or ≤1% planner/≥10× goal. No post-result tuning on the final-DEV panel.
