# V19 prospective method proposal — time-resolved matching deficit

Status: **pre-implementation** after independently audited V18 DEV no-go and first-divergence diagnosis; no V19 RL, smoke, DEV or TEST outcome has been read. Original goal gates remain intact.

## Problem and evidence

V18 bounded actor cost 876.57 versus rollout 857.23, with +70.95 cost/+3 H failures in interchangeable. At its first difference from rollout, own-actor H64 root values nearly tie (−0.79 CI95[−2.32,+0.65]) but risk-continuation values favor rollout (+14.01 [+7.37,+21.45]); 29/80 first differences are assignment labels whose risk root value ties exactly. V18/V14 reciprocal3 crossed roots first agree 270/400 times; V18 is more eager to serve/higher cardinality in 60/400, associated descriptively with expensive downstream trajectories. These do **not** prove an individual root is harmful. They motivate representing the future capacity implied by each *current full matching* over the whole service window, so repeated actor decisions can learn the opportunity cost of commitments. V14's simultaneous unserved-demand identity term failed its matched-capacity ablation; V15 exact hazard screen also failed. V19 therefore needs a stricter temporal ablation and must not relabel either as success.

## Core formulation and mechanism

Use the unchanged 56-dimensional current observation, threshold requester, legal partial matchings including WAIT, H48+16 risk tail, and economic cost. For each candidate matching `a`, compute a **deterministic, present-information** nine-tick profile (maximum current service duration is 9). At future offset `τ=1…9`, let `R_a(τ)` be currently queued non-served machines plus currently operating non-served machines whose observed age reaches the fixed PM-request threshold by `τ`. Machines already undergoing service or selected by `a` are excluded from this no-new-decision exposure approximation. Let `J_a(τ)` be technicians projected free after their current remaining service and the duration of service assigned by `a`. Compatibility comes from current known service-time edges. Define

`D_a(τ)=|R_a(τ)|/4`, `L_a(τ)=(|R_a(τ)|−ν(R_a(τ),J_a(τ)))/4`,

where `ν` is the maximum feasible bipartite matching cardinality. Thus `L` counts requests that could not all be started at that offset given the currently committed worker releases. It is an **exposure feature**, not a hard action filter, oracle Q, cost term, or claim to predict the stochastic future. New failures, future learned dispatches and repeat requests after service are outside this approximation. At inference, the actor receives the current observation, candidate incidence/duration/release information as in V17, and the two 9-element profiles, and scores **all** legal matchings. It learns from scratch using on-policy full-H64 common-tape Q ranks; no imitation/pretraining/fine-tuning or forecast simulation at inference. Joint action choice lets the actor trade immediate service against future shared capacity.

The controlled `snapshot_deficit` arm uses the **identical** actor architecture, parameters, current observation, every `D_a(τ)` and training/evaluation budget, but repeats `L_a(1)` in its nine deficit slots. It has present demand, ages, worker release, durations, action incidence and explicit future demand profile; the only removed structured input is the *action-specific time-resolved capacity deficit*. All other training elements (source states, common forecast scenarios, full matching legal set, rank objective, conditional assignment auxiliary, KL constraint) match. On-policy trajectories may diverge; log them. The exact profile is independent of learned weights, and the network may ignore it; the ablation tests whether temporal resource coupling contributes after information/capacity controls. Include V14 joint/count, V18 bounded/own, V7 flat/shared, FCFS, risk and rollout on the new physical panel. If a simple risk rule or fixed-duration policy dominates cost and runtime, do not promote the method.

## Falsifiable predictions and prior-work boundary

First, profile values must distinguish actions beyond the snapshot in a material fraction of legal-multi-action opened-DEV states; otherwise retire before full. Second, candidate should reduce PM queue/failure excess in interchangeable without a V11-style WAIT/CM collapse, improve paired equal-case H64 cost across strong and weak training seeds against `snapshot_deficit`, and approach or beat rollout while retaining ≥10× lower selection time. An architectural counterexample is that actor continuation remains self-confirming despite a better exposure feature, or `D(τ)` alone suffices, producing no ablation gain. No novelty claim follows from this proposal. Structured joint-action RL and global demand are established in Learning to Dispatch and AirME/HetGPO; V14 already used current unserved machine–worker competition; GPI and conservative Q improvement are established. The possible narrower distinction is action-dependent **future feasible matching deficit as a learned full-matching policy input** under persistent requests and heterogeneous service occupancy, if fresh matched ablations and competitive replication support it. Check closer time-indexed resource-shadow-price literature before any final novelty claim.
