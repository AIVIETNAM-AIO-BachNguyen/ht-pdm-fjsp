# Frozen Exchange PPO v1 allocation diagnostic protocol

Registered after the completed negative pilot, before diagnostic code/execution.
The pilot fails6/25 point screens and does not authorize fullDEV/confirmation.
This analysis reuses opened DEV solely to explain outcomes. No training, teacher,
labels, optimizer, learned warm start, objective changes, extra simulator branches,
scientific tests or new seeds. Every selected checkpoint remains immutable.

Hypotheses: N4 availability/cost loss may associate with duration/restoration
choices, leaving urgent legal capacity unused, or resource allocation to at-risk
machines. Frequent exchange events may produce little net change because
internal moves can undo earlier choices. These are hypotheses, not causal
diagnoses. Static construction/final contrasts do not predict counterfactual
returns or whether a different service would prevent a failure.

Grid: both selected replicas, all16 already opened DEV seeds, every registered
method/size at N4/N32, plus deadline once per size/seed. Exactly480 episodes,
28800 independently replayed physical policy+tail ticks. Smoke: first existing
replica and DEV seed, every method/size plus deadline:16 episodes/960 ticks.
Original48 decisions and12-tick deadline tail stay unchanged. Stop at complete
grid or technical failure; hard1200s limit without extension or metric stopping.

Record every original episode metric, starts/ages/durations/restorations,
preventive/corrective mix, policy service/failed-waiting/unavailable ticks,
residual free legal edges, unserved at-risk machines and their subsequent
failures with/without a residual free legal technician, conflicts/rejections.
For exchange/self-repair/construct-only, decode the construction prefix followed
by KEEP and the full trace at the same current state. Record both provisional
matchings, net matching changes, exchange activity with unchanged final matching,
cardinality/duration/restoration differences and at-risk coverage differences.
This uses two deterministic broker decodes, not a physical simulator fork or
an improvement oracle. Other arms have no comparable two-pass feasible matching;
mark these trace-only statistics inapplicable rather than interpreting zeros.

Verify all441 pilot output SHA/size bindings, current frozen source/PLAN/registry
identity, selected checkpoint metadata/digests and no parameter changes. Require
exact seed/method/replica grid, paired initial physical population hashes,
original metric reconciliation within1e-8, and independent scalar replay of
every physical transition/cost/component and broker/action reconciliation.

Each run creates a fresh UTC artifact directory containing copied script/plan,
manifest with input/script/plan/output bindings, decisions.csv, starts.csv,
physical.csv, episodes.csv and aggregate.json. Use tqdm. Syntax/diff checks,
relevant small correctness checks and the local lab smoke precede commit/push.
After pushing verified code, show the exact compound full diagnostic command
then execute directly here under the user override. No learning source,
registered learning protocol or registry is edited. No scientific alpha spent.
