This suggests early optimization with the selected-prefix path can destabilize training or checkpoint choice. B5 tests whether introducing the B4 selected-prefix attention, capacity inputs and direct residual only after a common mask-only precursor improves the matched full-versus-mask-only contrast. The proposed training schedule is the sole change to the graph policy. Do not treat the same-weight diagnosis as evidence of a positive matched effect.
