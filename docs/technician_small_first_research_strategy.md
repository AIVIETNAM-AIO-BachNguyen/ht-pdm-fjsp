# Research sequence: solve N4/K2 before generalization

Active user steering on 2026-10-16: concentrate algorithm design on four machines
and two technicians; establish success there before studying generalization.
This replaces the earlier practice of including N32 in each exploratory pilot.
The overall teacher-free coordination, quality, health and eventual scaling
objective remains unchanged. This document sets the research sequence; it does
not register a new algorithm, seed panel, test or experiment.

## Current evidence and preserved limits

Exchange v2 is a completed negative pilot. Its frozen diagnostic is complete;
there is no live v2 training or diagnostic process. Its failure does not authorize
development, confirmation, a teacher rescue, or a claim that the research goal
has been achieved. All older scarcity/completion/exchange results stay intact.
No previously unopened scientific panel has been opened for this new sequence.

The N4 diagnostic finds extra service occupancy, failed waiting and poor at-risk
coverage relative to normalized PPO. These are descriptive associations. Prior
scarcity work already tested opportunity prices and hierarchical stopping;
completion work tested calendar/prefix credit; exchange tested local repair and
commitment. Repeating or renaming these mechanisms is not a new contribution.
Generic learned ordering and conflict arbitration also have relevant prior art.

## First establish small-setting algorithm quality

The next prospective algorithm and experiments focus on N4/K2 only. No new
N8/N16/N32 training or generalization run starts until the N4 success gate is
verified. Spend the design and experimental effort on a precise coordination
or learning mechanism, trained from random weights using its own returns.
PPO may supply the optimizer; there is no baseline checkpoint, distillation,
teacher label, imitation stage or PPO warm start.

Keep original PPO, normalized PPO, current local MAPPO, full-information MAPPO
and an ablation of the proposed mechanism. Use the same physical information
and matching training budgets for causal mechanism comparisons, match core
architecture/capacity/initialization where applicable, and disclose external
baseline differences. An extra-information win over local MAPPO is insufficient.
All replicas and failed runs remain in the record.

Before each code experiment, register the exact hypothesis, action semantics,
likelihood/objective, metrics, fresh history-disjoint train/DEV/test seeds,
checkpoint selection, sample/optimizer budgets, stopping rule, statistical family
and artifact schema. Use focused tests and a small local lab smoke, then commit
and push verified code, show the exact compound command, and run directly on
the lab under the active override. Keep tqdm and fresh timestamped artifacts.

Exploratory N4 pilots can reject weak mechanisms cheaply. They cannot establish
success. A small-setting success claim requires prelocked full-budget evaluation
on new seeds with uncertainty: near centralized PPO (retain the -1% practical
noninferiority margin), improvement over MAPPO under the registered fair
information comparison, and a beneficial matched mechanism ablation. Preserve
the existing health limits relative to PPO: availability difference at least
-0.01, pending/machine difference at most +0.025, failures/machine difference at
most +0.25; zero invalid assignments. Any superiority-over-PPO claim still needs
the separately prelocked +1% practical gain and its confidence bound.

Lock the precise simultaneous confidence family, alpha accounting and mechanism
margin in the next algorithm-specific protocol before outcomes, preserving the
unused confirmation allocation and historical accounting. Do not reuse a failed
round's test panel or treat exploratory checkpoint selection as confirmation.

## Then study transfer and computational scaling

After verified N4 success, register a separate scale/generalization study.
Distinguish transfer of the N4 policy to unseen instances or larger sizes without
retraining from training a new policy at each size. The latter measures scaling
and must not be labeled zero-shot generalization. Test cost, failures,
availability, backlog and measured decision time together.

The candidate representation should remain compatible with a polynomial
coordination procedure. At N4, enumerating the 21 dense feasible matchings is
useful for baseline policies and correctness checks; making that catalogue the
new candidate would abandon the eventual nonenumeration objective. Efficient
scaling remains a later requirement, not a reason to divert the current
experimental budget from solving N4/K2.
