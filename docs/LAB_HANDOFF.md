## What the existing evidence establishes

The completed run is `maintenance_request_lifecycle_v1_full_cpu_20261019T141931Z`:
3,456 episodes, 221,184 transitions, zero invalid assignments. Independent
scalar replay and reconstruction of all 54 means / 45 registered contrasts
passed. The rollout forecast bank was not completely rerun by the analysis.

At the threshold/reciprocal_scale2 anchor, mean cost is 885.20 FCFS, 806.16 risk
matching and 717.69 rollout. Rollout/risk gain is 10.97%, with improvements in
availability and CM waiting. These are development observations on opened
seeds, not a fresh confirmatory claim or evidence RL will win.

Across all nine profiles rollout loses to risk in two. A different profile
reduces cost while losing substantial availability. Early free submission often
changes request timestamps without changing physical schedules. Do not hide
negative results, claim intrinsic submission-timing tradeoffs, attribute all
gains to heterogeneous assignment, or claim optimality from these heuristics.
See [prior paper positioning](maintenance_framework_positioning_vi.md).
